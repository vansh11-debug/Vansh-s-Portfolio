Overview

At a glance

Runway is a fashion shopping app that offers a curated selection of trendy clothing and accessories. With an intuitive, user-friendly design, customers can easily browse, shop, and stay ahead of fashion trends. Secure payments and seamless order tracking ensure a hassle-free shopping experience.

Problem

The challenge

Fashion-forward shoppers often struggle to find trendy, high-quality clothing in one place. Scattered options, unreliable sizing, and a lack of curated selections make shopping overwhelming and time-consuming.

👉 Finding trendy, high-quality fashion in one place is challenging — making shopping time-consuming and overwhelming for users who want to stay ahead of the curve.

Solution

What I designed

Runway simplifies fashion shopping by offering a curated selection of trendy clothing and accessories — all in one place, with secure payments and easy navigation for a seamless experience.

01
Shop from a Vast Selection

Explore a vast selection of trendy fashion pieces, from top brands to emerging designers, all in one place.

02
Aesthetic & User-Friendly Interface

With its aesthetic design and user-friendly interface, navigating through the app is both enjoyable and effortless.

03
Free Shipping

Enjoy free shipping on all orders above ₹990, making your shopping experience even more rewarding.

User Research

Understanding the user

Through supervised user research, I created the persona of Vivek — a 24-year-old freelance graphic designer and social media influencer with a passion for fashion. He's frustrated by the overwhelming number of online options that fail to meet his need for high-quality, curated fashion.

I also mapped out Vivek's user journey to better illustrate the difficulty he faces when trying to find trendy, high quality fashion.

Vivek's 5-stage user journey map

User story: As a trendsetting fashion enthusiast, I want a seamless way to discover and purchase the latest fashion trends

Usability Study

What users said

I conducted a usability testing session with 5 participants and ran the responses through a card sorting exercise to find common themes. These insights directly shaped the final hi-fi design.

Usability study findings across Measurement, New Habits, and Design categories

Final Design

The final prototype

After incorporating usability feedback, I refined the design into a polished high-fidelity prototype showcasing the full Runway experience.

Final hi-fi screens — Homepage with categories and Men's fashion browsing view

Reflection

What I learned

Creating Runway reinforced the importance of understanding user needs through research and how personas guide product decisions. I learned how crucial intuitive design is when users have high expectations around aesthetics — especially in fashion.

I also gained insights into balancing visual appeal and functionality — delivering a product that is both beautiful and genuinely usable.
