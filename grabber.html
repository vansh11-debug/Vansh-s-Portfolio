Problem

The challenge

Busy individuals often struggle to find time for grocery shopping due to hectic schedules. Traditional shopping can be time-consuming and inconvenient, leading to skipped meals, unhealthy takeout, and constant stress about running out of essentials.

🚀 The goal was to create a fast, convenient way to order fresh groceries online — saving time and effort for customers who simply don't have the bandwidth for store visits.

Solution

What I designed

Grabber provides a seamless, efficient shopping experience — from browsing fresh groceries to real-time delivery tracking, all from one app.

01
All Your Essentials in One Place

Browse from a wide range of fresh groceries — fruits, vegetables, dairy, and pantry essentials — all available at your fingertips.

02
Hassle-Free Checkout

Enjoy fast, secure, and hassle-free payments with multiple trusted payment options including UPI and card.

03
Stay Updated on Your Delivery

Track your orders in real time and stay updated on your delivery status every step of the way.

User Research

Understanding the user

Through supervised user research, I developed the persona of Priya — a busy marketing manager who struggles to balance her demanding work schedule with grocery shopping. She often finds herself running out of essentials but lacks the time for frequent store visits.

I also mapped out Priya's user journey to better illustrate the difficulty she faces when trying to purchase groceries.

Priya's 5-stage user journey map

Usability Study

What users said

I conducted a usability testing session with 5 participants and ran the responses through a card sorting exercise to find common themes. The insights directly shaped the final hi-fi design.

Measurement

40% felt search & filter options could be improved for easier product discovery.

Measurement

30% found checkout slightly slow and preferred a quick buy option for repeat orders.

Measurement

40% suggested adding a delivery time tracker for more accurate arrival estimates.

New Habits

60% indicated AI-powered smart shopping lists based on past purchases are useful and time-saving.

New Habits

50% suggested a subscription feature for essentials, so they never run out of key items.

Design

80% liked the simple, clean UI — saying it makes shopping efficient and stress-free.

Reflection

What I learned

Creating Grabber taught me the importance of understanding user needs through research and how user personas guide product decisions. I discovered how crucial it is to focus on intuitive design and seamless user experiences to meet user expectations.

I also gained insights into balancing aesthetics and functionality — delivering a product that is both visually appealing and highly usable.
