Overview

At a glance

Our dog walker app connects busy pet owners with trusted, on-demand walkers to ensure their dogs get the care and exercise they need. With flexible booking options, customers can schedule one-time or recurring walks, browse walker profiles, check ratings, and make secure payments — all with a few taps.

Problem

The challenge

Busy professionals and pet owners struggle to find reliable and convenient dog-walking services that fit their schedules. Traditional options are often inconsistent, lack transparency, or require long-term commitments — leaving pet owners worried about their dogs not getting enough exercise and care.

👉 There is a need for a user-friendly platform that connects pet owners with trusted, on-demand dog walkers who provide safe and flexible services.

Solution

What I designed

This app provides busy pet owners with a reliable and convenient way to find trusted dog walkers on demand — with flexible booking, secure payments, and peace of mind built right in.

01
Select from Trusted Dog Walkers

Browse a wide range of trusted dog walkers with detailed profiles, giving you the flexibility to choose the best fit for your pet's needs.

02
Recurring Booking

Schedule multiple dog walks in advance with our recurring booking feature. Set daily, weekly, or custom schedules for a hassle-free experience.

03
Choose Based on Verified Ratings

Customers can easily choose their ideal dog walker based on detailed ratings and reviews — ensuring a trustworthy and reliable experience every time.

User Research

Understanding the user

Through supervised user research, I created the persona of Abhay — a 22-year-old recent graduate who just landed his first job. His days are packed with work, leaving little time to walk his dog. He's explored a few dog-walking services but hasn't found one that's flexible, reliable, and convenient enough.

I also mapped out Abhay's user journey to illustrate the difficulty he faces when trying to select a dog-walking service.

Abhay's journey map — Actions, Tasks, Emotions and Improvement Opportunities

User story: As a busy professional, I want to schedule dog walks so my dog gets exercise even when I don't have time

Usability Study

What users said

I conducted a usability testing session with 5 participants and ran the responses through a card sorting exercise to find common themes. These insights directly shaped the final hi-fi design.

Final Design

The final prototype

After incorporating usability study insights, I refined the design into a polished high-fidelity prototype — showcasing the complete booking experience from browsing walkers to confirming appointments.

Final hi-fi screens — Walker listing and appointment confirmation

Reflection

What I learned

Creating Dogwalker reinforced the importance of understanding user needs through research and how personas guide product decisions. I learned how trust and transparency are especially critical in service-based apps — users need to feel confident before handing over their pet.

I also gained insights into balancing aesthetics and functionality — delivering a product that is both visually appealing and genuinely usable for busy, time-strapped users.
